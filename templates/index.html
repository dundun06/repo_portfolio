About Me

Hello! I am Nguyen Thi Dung, a passionate Literature Tutor dedicated to helping students discover the beauty of literature and excel in their academic pursuits.

With a strong background in Literature from Bac Giang High School for the Gifted and currently an undergraduate student at Foreign Trade University, I have honed my skills and knowledge to guide students effectively.

My teaching philosophy revolves around personalized learning, fostering a comfortable environment, and ensuring that every student can confidently express their thoughts and achieve outstanding results.
